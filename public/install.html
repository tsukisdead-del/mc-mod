Option 1 Install profile

  1. Download install profile When Safari asks to download a configuration profile, tap Allow, then Close.
  2. Open the Settings app and tap Profile Downloaded near the top. (If you don't see it: Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.)
  3. Tap Install, enter your iPhone passcode, then tap Install again. It shows "Not Verified" because it's your own personal profile and not signed by a company. That's expected.
  4. Go to your Home Screen and open Dr. Mochi. Type your access code.
  5. In the app, open Settings (top right) and turn on Notifications so Dr. Mochi can text you first.

The profile only adds a Home Screen icon. It doesn't manage, track, or change anything else on your phone. Remove it any time from Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

Notifications: Apple only promises check-in notifications for apps added from Safari (option 2). If this version never offers to turn them on, add it the option 2 way too and use that one.

Option 2 Add to Home Screen

The quick way, and the one Apple supports for notifications (iOS 16.4 or newer):

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

If you used the profile and the app never asks for notification permission, delete that icon and add it this way instead. Notifications need iOS 16.4 or newer.
